Visualizer stream (Sendspin)

Lets Music Assistant stream live visualizer data (loudness, spectrum, beats) to the board, so the Music visualizer app follows the music that's playing. The board joins as a display-only Sendspin client: it plays no audio and uses no microphone. It needs pip install -r requirements-sendspin.txt on the server (Python 3.12+, and the version must match Music Assistant's). Without it, the visualizer uses a generated beat.

auto finds Music Assistant on the network. You can also enter ws://IP:8927/sendspin, or leave it empty to wait for Music Assistant to find the board.
